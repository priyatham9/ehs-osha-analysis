FROM FINDING TO REVIEW RULE

One record is enough to reverse this comparison.

Apply the same single-deletion check to every included record in both years. Route a sign-changing record for review; retain it until its units and reporting scope can be verified.

The operational rule is defined here and applied retrospectively. It was not historically preregistered. No single-record reversal does not establish robustness to combined deletions.

Separate within-group change from changing composition

Peer groups are industry × establishment-size cells, not a longitudinal panel of the same establishments. This decomposition is descriptive. The residual accounts for changes in cell support and renormalization.

Symmetric decomposition: within = Σ mean(weight) × Δrate; composition = Σ mean(rate) × Δweight. Full change = within + composition + support residual. These are algebraic contributions, not causal effects.
