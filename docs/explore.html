Read before you quote it

What this can and cannot tell you

Can

  • Show where one site-year's TRIR sits against a public, self-reported peer distribution for a NAICS-3 industry and size band, after the hours screen.
  • Show whether a set of hours and headcount would pass the pipeline's plausibility screen.
  • Show how much the aggregate rate across all ITA filers depends on screening implausible hours first.

Cannot

  • Stand in for the BLS national rate, OSHA's enforcement data or a regulator's view. ITA covers establishments required to file electronically, not every workplace. This is Form 300A summary data only, not inspections or citations.
  • Prove any single filing is accurate. The screen catches implausible hours; it cannot verify case counts, classification or intent.
  • Match peers more closely than NAICS-3 and one of seven size bands. Two sites in the same cell can differ a great deal.
  • Give an exact percentile. The table stores p10, p25, p50, p75, p90 and p95, so a rate is placed between two of them, not interpolated.
  • Say why a site ranks where it does. A percentile is an association with self-reported peers, not a cause.
  • Treat a small cell as reliable. Cells under 30 filings are marked below the publishing threshold; read them with caution.
